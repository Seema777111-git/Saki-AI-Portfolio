02Problem and solution

The business problem, solved

🎯 The problem

Scheme information is spread across government pages written in official language, mostly in English. Many farmers never learn about subsidies that exist for them, or which documents they need.

💡 The solution

  • Multilingual: English, Tamil and Tanglish
  • Official data only: from the TN Agrisnet portal, with the source cited
  • Hybrid retrieval: meaning-based and keyword search, then reranking
  • Guardrails: refuse off-topic or unclear questions

04How it works

Under the hood

How RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) works here: the AI answers only from evidence it retrieves.

Key RAG concepts

🔎Hybrid retrievalCombines meaning-based search with keyword search, so both phrasing and exact scheme names are found.
🔤BM25A keyword ranking method that rewards exact matches such as scheme names.
🏅RerankingA cross-encoder reads each candidate passage with the question and keeps the most relevant.
🏠Local LLMQwen 2.5 runs locally through Ollama to write the final answer.
🛡️GuardrailsOff-topic or unclear questions are refused instead of guessed.
🗣️MultilingualQuestions in English, Tamil or Tanglish reach the same official data.
