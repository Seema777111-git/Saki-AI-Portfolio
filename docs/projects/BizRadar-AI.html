02Problem and solution

The business problem, solved

🎯 The problem

Before a new business launches, someone has to study the competition. A marketing intern typically spends 2 to 3 days searching websites, maps and reviews and copying it into Excel. The result has no sources and goes out of date quickly.

💡 The solution

  • Five AI agents: Manager, Market Researcher, Review Analyst, Strategy Analyst, Report Writer
  • Proof for every claim: each price links to its source page and date
  • Honest labels: Fact, Estimate, Inference or Unknown
  • Built for India: Google Maps, Justdial, Swiggy, Zomato, IndiaMART and more, prices in ₹
  • Never overspends: hard limits on tokens, pages, searches and time
