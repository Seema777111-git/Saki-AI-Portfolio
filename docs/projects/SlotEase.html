02Problem and solution

The business problem, solved

🎯 The problem

Appointment-based businesses take bookings by phone and WhatsApp. Two customers get the same slot, staff forget to record bookings, and there is no record of no-shows.

💡 The solution

  • A booking page per business: in its own colours
  • No double bookings: the database decides in one transaction
  • Owner dashboard: today, next customer, one-tap Done or No-show
  • Working hours with breaks: with clinic and salon presets
  • Each business sealed off: and this is tested
