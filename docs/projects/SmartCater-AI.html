02Problem and solution

The business problem, solved

🎯 The problem

Caterers receive enquiries on WhatsApp, Telegram, phone and forms. Messages are missed, quotations are typed by hand and follow-ups are forgotten, so bookings go to faster competitors.

💡 The solution

  • AI chat agent: answers questions and collects event type, date, guests, venue, food and budget
  • One lead database: in Supabase with a clear stage for every enquiry
  • Instant follow-up: confirmation email and a calendar invitation
  • Owner approval: before any price goes out
  • Itemised quotation PDF: with GST, emailed automatically
