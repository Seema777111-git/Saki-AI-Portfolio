02Problem and solution

The business problem, solved

🎯 The problem

Small business owners have sales data in spreadsheets but no analyst. General AI chatbots can produce convincing numbers that are wrong, which is not acceptable for business decisions.

💡 The solution

  • The AI never does the maths: numbers come from executed calculations
  • Checked twice: a mismatch blocks the answer
  • Data Quality Guardian: explains duplicates and bad values first
  • Facts kept apart from guesses: labelled “not proven”
  • Human in control: nothing is final until approved

04How it works

Under the hood

Click the picture to open it full size.

  • Smart router: cached answers use no AI tokens, standard questions use a verified Python engine, and only custom questions use the full agent loop
  • AI agent team: Planner → Coder → Sandbox → Verifier → Reviewer → Advisor → human approval
  • Safe execution: AI-written code runs in a locked-down Docker sandbox with no internet access and strict limits
  • Security: bcrypt passwords, rotating refresh tokens, rate limiting and per-user data isolation
