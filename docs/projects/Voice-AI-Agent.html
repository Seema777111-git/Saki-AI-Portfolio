02Problem and solution

The business problem, solved

🎯 The problem

Customers increasingly send voice notes instead of typing. Jewellers, clinics and agents receive the same questions all day, often after hours, and cannot reply to every message quickly.

💡 The solution

  • Understands voice and text: with OpenAI Whisper
  • Uses live data: through Google search (SerpAPI)
  • Calculates reliably: with a calculator tool
  • Takes action: by sending emails through Gmail
  • Replies twice: full text plus a short spoken summary

04Responsible AI

Reliability safeguards

  • Only quotes a number that appears in the search results; otherwise it says so
  • Never reports an email as sent unless Gmail confirms it
  • Replies are escaped so special characters cannot block delivery
